WHY OHAMS EXISTS

One night changed everything.

Far from home, during his master's in the UK, our founder watched a young neighbour collapse alone in his room — and then watched the hours pass before help arrived. It wasn't the distance that failed him. It was the silence around him — the kind stigma builds quietly, long before any crisis.

"What if care had reached him first?"

Ohams Care is the answer we're building — a system where no one suffers alone because of stigma, distance, or money. Premium wellness funds free care. Privacy is architecture, not a feature.
